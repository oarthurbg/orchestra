# Lessons

Problems that happened once and must not happen again. One line each, newest first, with the date, the area it applies to and where it came from. Added only by the maestro, through a docs PR; a lesson that turns out wrong is deleted, not left behind. A one-off setup failure, a claim that a tool is broken, or a story stays out. A lesson seen again gets the new date added to its line, not a second line; one seen twice can be promoted to "Rules from lessons" in `CLAUDE.md` (maestro skill, "Lessons").
